Mission Narrative & Objectives

We Landed Roughly, For Reasons Still Unknown.

The mission protocol is clear: establish an emergency foothold, restore vital systems, and coordinate an evacuation window with orbit.

Phase 1

Stabilise the Crew

Reach the emergency Life Pod, treat physical trauma, and repair the damaged oxygen scrubber using salvaged filtration cores.

Phase 2

Build a Transmitter

Scavenge high-gain antennas and power supplies across crater rims and derelict rovers to broadcast a distress signal.

Phase 3

Call the Return Vehicle

Guide the Earth Return Vehicle to your exact planetary coordinates and initiate the automated ignition countdown.

Survival Mechanics

Every Resource Shapes a Player Decision

Oxygen
Drains faster outside and spikes during jetpack bursts, forcing you to calculate every expedition's turning point before leaving shelter.
Power
Follows real-time solar elevation angles, compelling players to stockpile energy during peak noon or face life-support brownouts at night.
Water
Depletes steadily with metabolic activity, requiring risky treks into glacial mountain basins to extract cryogenic ice samples.
Food
Caloric drain sets an unyielding operational deadline on Sol progression, penalising wasted hours and uncalculated detours.
